How it is built.One model, three steps.

Start from a base model1 / 3

Training a model to be helpful is what makes it stop acting like a person, so we start before that step, from the full range of how people talk.

Questionsit can answer.

The kinds of questions we are building it for, once training is complete.

  • How do impatient users react when an agent asks for one more step?Where do confused users give up, and what do they say when they do?Which wording makes a delay feel fair rather than evasive?
  • How hard will a tenant push back on a renewal price?Which offer gets a counterparty to sign for longer?When does holding firm end the conversation?
  • Does the same message land differently by age, culture or temperament?Which customers leave after a price change, and which stay?
  • Would a person find this reply pushy, cold or helpful?Which of two agents feels more trustworthy, and why?
