      The models

      One person. A crowd. An institution.

      Three models on one foundation, each answering the same kind of question at a different scale.

      01 · One person

      Judgment

      How a particular person would choose, given who they are and what is in front of them.

      In
      A person and a set of options
      Out
      Their choice, a score for each option, and why
      Ask itWill this candidate accept the offer, or push back?
      02 · A crowd

      Prediction

      How a population will respond. The answer is a spread of reactions, the way real groups split.

      In
      A population and a change
      Out
      How the group divides, and the numbers behind it
      Ask itIf the price goes up 20%, how many subscribers leave?
      03 · An institution

      Adjudication

      How a body of people deliberates and rules. Several voices argue; the ruling follows from the debate.

      In
      A dispute and the arguments on each side
      Out
      A ruling and the reasoning behind it
      Ask itWould an arbitration panel say this delivery met the contract?
